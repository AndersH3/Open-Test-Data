# Third-party dataset notices

The repository's AGPL licence applies to original project contributions. **It does not replace any third-party dataset, stimulus, codebook or documentation licence.** Each collection retains its original attribution and applicable terms. Full licence texts and source records are stored in the linked folders.

| Collection | Dataset licence | Additional scope/conditions |
|---|---|---|
| [SAPA ability and iqitems](datasets/sapa-ability-iqitems/) | GPL 2.0 or later | Exact packaged response source objects, keys and author codebooks under the formal psychTools package licence. The current ICAR bank and separate stimuli are not included. |
| [Fraction subtraction](datasets/fraction-subtraction/) | GPL 2.0 or later | Exact CDM response and Q-matrix source objects, author documentation and package grant. No question facsimiles or artwork. |
| [ECPE grammar](datasets/ecpe-grammar/) | GPL 2.0 or later | Exact CDM response/Q-matrix source object and author codebook. No proprietary ECPE questions, forms or handbook. |
| [Ganis-Kievit mental rotation](datasets/ganis-kievit-mental-rotation/) | CC BY 4.0 | Original behavioural archive and deposited JPG stimulus bank; cite the authors and versioned Figshare record. Duplicate image encodings and older archives are omitted explicitly. |
| [Self-Regulation Ontology](datasets/sro-performance-tasks/) | CC BY 4.0 | Objective-task subset only; original per-task members retained with commit and member hashes. Survey/preference tasks are excluded. Separate experimental-code/stimulus rights are not generalized. |
| [OpenNeuro ds004636](datasets/openneuro-ds004636-objective-tasks/) | CC0 1.0 | Objective behavioural event files and original BIDS sidecars. MRI, surveys and preference tasks are outside the mirror. Study exclusions remain the analyst's responsibility. |

Do not distribute cleaned, merged or otherwise adapted response tables under this mirror's permission. |

Source-specific attributions, citations, version identifiers and acquisition paths are in each `SOURCE.md` and manifest. A project-authored field guide is labelled as such; it does not claim author approval or add permissions to original material. Retain the relevant notices and licence text when redistributing any source files.

The [37-source redistribution audit](data/redistribution-audit.json) records sources excluded from the mirror. Public availability or a paper's licence alone is not a data grant. Formal package-wide GPL grants can cover packaged response objects when no contrary notice applies; they do not authorize separate proprietary test banks or stimuli. No restricted-use applications or custom access agreements were submitted on the user's behalf.
